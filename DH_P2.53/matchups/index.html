How expected FPA works

Defense ranks. Within the selected period, rank 1 allows the fewest PPR points per game at the selected position. Ties share a rank. BYEs are excluded and missing results stay unavailable.

Expected FPA and SOS. Each game uses the opposing offense’s average PPR scoring in the same period. Expected FPA per game averages those baselines across the defense’s recorded opponents, counting repeat meetings separately. SOS ranks that average from lowest (easiest) to highest. Offense ranks use total points scored in the period.

Weekly results. Recorded player scores and box scores drive the weekly chart and player tables. Search and the scoring threshold apply only to displayed player rows. Matchup Trends always compares the regular season against the interval from Week 7 through the last completed week.
